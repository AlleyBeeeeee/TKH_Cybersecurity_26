QUESTION 2: Why must a SOC analyst NEVER use "Bridged" mode when detonating unknown malware?
ANSWER: If malware is in a Bridged VM, it can see every other device on your Wi-Fi. It can send ARP requests to find your phone or other computers and try
to "hop" from the VM to your real devices. It can also try to pivot or spread laterally across the network, potentially infecting the host machine or other
connected systems.
